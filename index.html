A little nostalgia. A lot of possibility.NEON KEYS JS v1.1.0

Make some noise.

Click Enable audio, choose a sound, and play. The keys A W S E D F T G Y H U J K O L P ; play C4–E5. Use Z / X to shift octaves, Space for sustain, and Esc to release all notes. Pitch bends ±2 semitones; modulation adds vibrato. Pitch returns to center when released.

Turn on the arpeggiator and hold a chord. It plays sixteenth notes at the selected tempo. The demo plays an original four-chord progression using the selected sound. Touch supports multiple fingers; computer-keyboard rollover depends on your hardware.

New in 1.1: choose Silk circuit for PWM pads, Skyline brass for unison, or Velvet glide for mono legato. Hold one note and press another; release the newest key to return to the previous held note. Retrigger restarts the envelopes on overlapping notes; Legato keeps them flowing. Glide is active in mono mode. Cutoff and resonance respond while notes are held.

Changing presets, waveforms, unison layer count, or play mode clears held notes. Other oscillator edits apply to a new phrase. Unison uses more processing power; reduce the layer count on slower devices.

Built with the browser’s Web Audio API. Presets evoke classic ’80s textures; they are original patches, not exact recreations of particular hardware.
